Abstract

This project presents an image-based approach for non-destructive wheat spike volume estimation. RGB images are used to predict spike volume, with structured-light 3D scans serving as ground truth. The goal is to provide an efficient and scalable alternative to manual or destructive measurements, enabling high-throughput phenotyping of wheat spikes.
